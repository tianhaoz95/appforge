## Full Description
Forge functional, beautiful micro-apps instantly using the power of Gemini AI and MicroForge's 'Vibe Coding' engine.

MicroForge transforms your natural language descriptions into fully interactive applications. Whether you need a task manager, a data visualizer, or an AI-powered assistant, simply describe your vision and watch MicroForge forge it in seconds.

**Key Features:**
- **Forge Instantly:** Use natural language to create apps with Alpine.js and Tailwind CSS.
- **Smart Refinement:** AI analyzes logs and screenshots to automatically fix bugs and improve UI.
- **MicroForge Bridge:** Full access to on-device capabilities like Geolocation, Accelerometer, and Local Notifications.
- **Advanced Backend:** Add complex logic with Vanilla JavaScript backends and periodic background tasks.
- **App Vault:** Save and manage your collection of forged apps.
- **Hybrid AI:** Seamlessly switches between high-performance cloud models and efficient on-device AI.
- **Modern UI:** Responsive designs that look great on any device, with full light and dark mode support.

Join the future of software creation with MicroForge. No code, just vibes.
